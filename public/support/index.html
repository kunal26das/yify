Plus

Yify Plus

Support an independent app and unlock a few extras.

Open Yify Plus Get the Android app

  • No Yify ads in the Android app

    Yify shows an occasional full-screen ad on Android. Plus turns it off. YouTube and streaming services may still show their own ads.

  • Unlimited lists

    Without Plus you can keep 3 custom lists. Plus lifts the limit (fair use: up to 100 lists).

  • Your year in review

    Diary insights: titles watched, hours, top genres and your ratings, by month and year.

  • Accent themes

    Choose an accent color for the app.

Plans and billing

Plus is a Google Play subscription, billed monthly or yearly. The app shows each plan's price in your currency before you confirm. Renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Plus stays active until the end of the paid period. The full subscription terms are part of our Terms of Use.

How to get Plus

  • In the Android app: open the You tab, choose See Plus, pick a plan and subscribe. No account needed. Sign in later to use Plus on the web too.
  • On the web: the website doesn't sell Plus. Get Yify Plus on Google Play. Bought Plus on Android? Sign in with the same Google account you use in Yify, then choose Refresh status on the Plus page.

Restore, manage and cancel

  • Restore purchases in the Android app finds a Plus subscription bought with your Google Play account. Restoring moves Plus to the account you're signed in with, or to this device if you're signed out.
  • Manage subscription opens your subscription in Google Play, where you can change your payment method or cancel.
  • Payment pending? Plus unlocks when Google Play confirms it. You don't need to pay again.
  • Payment problem? Update your payment method in Google Play to keep Plus.

Yify does not host, stream or sell movies or TV shows, and Plus does not include any streaming subscription. To watch a title, use one of the services listed under Where to watch.
