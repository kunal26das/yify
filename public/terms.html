What Yify provides

Yify helps you discover movies and TV shows, watch their official trailers, see where they are available to stream, rent or buy in your country, and keep a diary of what you watched alongside a watchlist and your own lists.

Yify does not host, stream or sell movies or TV shows. To watch a title, use one of the services listed under Where to watch; those services have their own availability, prices and terms.

Catalog information, images, trailers and availability come from external sources, may be incomplete or out of date, and can change or disappear at any time. A listing gives you no right to a title. Use Yify lawfully and respect other people's rights.

Your account and your data

You can use Yify without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and syncs your watchlist, diary, lists and settings across your devices. Keep your Google account secure, because anyone who can use it can open your Yify account. Yify is not meant for children under 13.

What you save, such as your watchlist, diary entries, ratings, notes and lists, stays yours and private: Yify does not show it to other people. You let us store and sync it only so that we can provide the service. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in You › Settings › Account; see Delete your account. Deleting your account, signing out or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Yify Plus

Yify Plus is an optional subscription that supports an independent app and adds four benefits:

  • no Yify ads in the Android app;
  • unlimited lists, where the free version keeps 3 custom lists;
  • your year in review: diary insights by month and by year;
  • accent themes.

Plus list storage has a fair-use limit of 100 lists and 2,000 listed titles per account. Plus does not include any movie, TV show or streaming-service subscription, and YouTube and streaming services may still show their own ads.

You buy Plus through Google Play in the Android app. You don't need an account to buy it. If you sign in, Plus also unlocks on the website with the same account; the website itself does not sell Plus. Plus is billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app and in Google Play before you confirm, including any taxes Google Play applies, and Google Play's terms apply to the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them.

If Plus ends, nothing you saved is removed. You can still open, edit and delete every list and diary entry, but creating more than 3 lists, the year in review and accent themes need Plus again. A one-time purchase made in an earlier version of Yify keeps unlocking Plus; use Restore purchases in the app if it is missing.
