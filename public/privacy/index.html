1. Information we use

Accounts and saved activity

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication supplies an account identifier, name, email address and profile image. We use these to sign you in and associate your saved information with your account. We do not receive your Google password.

Your watchlist, viewing history, watched status, collection names and membership, and preferences (including your selected viewing country) can be stored on your device. When signed in, Yify also synchronizes this saved activity, settings and records of removals through Firebase so your devices can stay in sync. Recent searches and cached catalogue information are also stored locally.

If you use the private movie journal, Yify stores the movies you log, watch dates, your optional personal ratings and notes, and records of edits and removals. Journal records are associated with your signed-in account, cached separately on your device and synchronized through Firebase. They are not published to other users. Viewing insights are calculated on your device from these records. Your journal remains available to read, edit and delete if supporter access ends.

Usage and diagnostics

Google Analytics for Firebase receives usage events such as screens visited, searches and search terms, selected titles, playback and watchlist activity, notification opens, purchase activity and app preferences. Providers may also process device or browser information, IP addresses, installation or session identifiers, and technical usage information.

Journal analytics record actions such as opening the journal, saving or deleting an entry, and opening insights or supporter options. These journal events do not include your notes, personal ratings, watch dates or logged titles.

Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry help diagnose crashes and performance problems. Reports can include error messages, stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device information, timings and the actions leading to an error. Sentry also receives sampled performance traces and profiles, diagnostic logs and metrics. Some native analytics events are recorded as Crashlytics breadcrumbs. Yify filters information from Sentry reports, but diagnostic information should not be assumed to be anonymous.

If you send a problem report or contact support, we receive the message and any information you choose to include. The in-app problem-report form does not request your name or email address or attach screenshots. Please avoid including sensitive information in a report.

Purchases and support

RevenueCat processes customer identifiers, products, transaction and subscription status, and purchase history so Yify can validate purchases, restore access and manage supporter benefits. When you sign in, your Yify account identifier can be linked to your RevenueCat customer record. On supported native builds, a Firebase app-instance identifier may be shared with RevenueCat to connect purchase and advertising analytics.

The store or web checkout provider processes billing and payment details. Yify does not store complete payment-card numbers. An external tip through Buy Me a Coffee is handled by that service and does not unlock Yify features.

Notifications and external content

With your permission, Yify can check the catalogue in the background and create local notifications for new titles. Your operating-system and app preferences control these notifications.

Where the optional Watchlist availability pilot is offered, you can separately enable alerts for saved movies becoming available on your selected streaming services. This requires an eligible subscription and a signed-in account. Yify stores a device or browser push-delivery token, a device registration identifier, your time zone, viewing country and registration time with your Firebase account. Scheduled processing on GitHub uses these records, your synchronized watchlist, selected streaming services and notification preferences to check availability through TMDB and verify subscription access through RevenueCat. Backend availability requests identify titles and do not send your Yify account identifier or push token to TMDB.

These optional alerts use Expo, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on Apple devices, Apple’s push-notification service to deliver messages without an open Yify screen or tab, subject to device and browser support. Delivery providers process the destination token and notification content. Yify keeps availability observations and delivery records to detect changes, avoid repeated alerts and handle delivery failures. The browser also stores a bounded list of recent notification event identifiers to prevent duplicate displays.

Streaming-service choices are saved preferences, not connected streaming accounts. Yify does not collect streaming-service passwords. For availability supplied by JustWatch through TMDB, the app connects directly to TMDB, which receives requested title identifiers, country filters where applicable, and connection information such as your IP address. These requests do not include your Yify account credentials or streaming-service credentials. Viewing links open a regional TMDB page; following its provider links takes you to that service, where its own sign-in and privacy policy apply.

If you choose Use current location, Yify asks for location permission to detect your viewing country once. You can decline and select a country manually. Mobile apps use the operating system’s location and reverse-geocoding services; their providers may process the location needed to identify your country. On the web, Yify requests location with high accuracy disabled, rounds latitude and longitude to two decimal places, and sends those approximate coordinates and your connection IP address directly to BigDataCloud. BigDataCloud uses location/IP pairings to improve its geolocation data, as described in its policy. Yify saves only the resulting country preference, which can sync with your account; it does not save coordinates, include them in analytics or diagnostics, or track your location in the background. You can revoke location permission through your browser or device settings.

Catalogue, artwork and trailer requests connect to external providers. Those providers receive the connection information needed to respond, such as your IP address and the requested resource. YouTube may load an embedded trailer when the app displays or plays it, including when autoplay is enabled.

2. Service providers

We use information to operate Yify, synchronize your account, deliver purchases and advertising, understand usage, fix problems, provide support and protect the service. Providers process information needed for these functions:

  • Google and Firebase: authentication, cloud storage, usage analytics, crash reporting, advertising, Google Play purchases, push-notification delivery and YouTube content.
  • RevenueCat and Stripe: purchase validation, subscription management, web checkout and related analytics.
  • Sentry: errors, performance measurements and user-submitted problem reports.
  • Expo: app updates, website hosting and optional push-notification delivery.
  • GitHub: website hosting and scheduled processing for optional availability alerts. Hosting and processing can produce ordinary delivery and server logs.
  • Apple: operating-system services and push-notification delivery on Apple devices.
  • Catalogue and media services, including YTS-compatible catalogue providers, EZTV, TMDB, image-delivery services and YouTube.

External stores, websites and services also have their own policies. We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect users and the service. If the service changes ownership, any transfer of information will remain subject to applicable privacy obligations and notice requirements.

4. Your choices

You can use catalogue features without signing in. In Preferences, you can manage saved history, watchlist data, playback and notification settings. You can also clear local browser or app data using your device's controls.

You can turn off Watchlist availability under Preferences → Notifications on each device or browser where you enabled it. Disabling alerts or signing out requests removal of that device’s enrollment and revocation of its push delivery. If the device is offline, cleanup may need to wait until you reconnect. You can also block notifications through your operating-system or browser settings. Changing these alert settings does not cancel your subscription.

To delete your Yify account, use Preferences → Account → Delete account, or follow the account-deletion instructions. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of other personal information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Depending on your location, applicable law may also give you rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Contact us to exercise applicable rights or ask how they apply to your information.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and synchronized data are kept to provide your account and cross-device features until you delete them or your account. Successful account deletion removes the Firebase account and the cloud content synchronized by Yify, including journal entries and availability-alert enrollments. A minimal deletion marker associated with the former account identifier is retained to prevent another signed-in device from restoring deleted journal content. It contains no journal entries, titles, dates, ratings or notes. The deleting device clears its journal cache; other devices clear their copies when they next successfully synchronize. Copies on devices that cannot synchronize can remain until local app or browser data is cleared. Scheduled alert processing clears delivery state when it detects an unenrolled device or a deleted account. Device-only data must be cleared separately.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel a subscription, refund a payment, or delete every record held by billing, advertising, analytics or diagnostics providers. Transaction records may be retained for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, refund and legal obligations. Usage, diagnostic, support and delivery records are retained according to their purpose, provider configuration and applicable requirements. Contact us about additional deletion requests.

Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and other locations in which they operate. Applicable provider agreements and data-protection requirements govern that processing.
