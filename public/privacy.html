Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information Yify uses, why it is used, who receives it and the choices you have.

It covers the Yify app for Android and the website at yify.expo.app, including the API that serves both. Yify is operated by Kunal Das, using the name Kudos Labs, who is responsible for the personal information it processes. Contact us about this policy or your information at kunal26das@gmail.com. Correspondence address: Kunal Das, C-92/93 Chhatrapati NAGAR, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh 462041, India.

In short

  • You can use Yify without an account. Your watchlist, diary and lists stay on your device unless you sign in to sync them.
  • Usage analytics is off until you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Crash and error reports are always on. They are filtered before they leave your device, and our crash-reporting provider does not store your IP address.
  • The Android app shows an occasional Google ad to people without Yify Plus. The website shows no ads.
  • Catalog data reaches you through our own API. Trailers play from YouTube, which receives your IP address and what you watch.

1. What we use and why

Your library and settings

Your watchlist, your diary of what you watched (dates, ratings, notes and rewatches), your lists, your recently viewed titles, your recent searches and your settings (theme, accent color, country, streaming services and trailer captions) are stored on your device: in the app's storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the website. Without an account we do not receive them. The app uses some of them to build requests to our API, such as your country, the streaming services you filter by and the titles whose streaming availability it shows (see Catalog, images and where to watch).

If you used Yify 1.8 or earlier on the same device, the update converts the library, journal, history and settings that version saved into the new format when it first opens, and deletes the old copies 30 days later. Titles saved by those versions were identified by the old catalog, so to match them the app sends their IMDb IDs, or their old IDs with their names and years, to our API. Our API looks them up at TMDB and, until the old catalog is retired, at the third-party catalog service those versions used. No account information is sent.

Your account and sync

Signing in is optional. You sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication, which gives us your account ID, name, email address and profile photo. We never receive your Google password. On the website, the sign-in pages are served from yify.expo.app and passed on to Firebase.

While you are signed in, your watchlist, diary, lists, recently viewed titles and synced settings (theme, accent color, country, streaming services, trailer captions and whether recently viewed is paused) are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account, so they are the same on every device where you sign in. Recent searches, your privacy choices and cached catalog data stay on each device. Nothing you save is shown to other people.

If you used Yify 1.8 or earlier with the same Google account, the app copies the watchlist, history, collections, journal and settings that version saved in your account into the new format. While devices on those versions are still in use, it checks for changes they made, at most once an hour, and copies those too. You can run the import again from You › Settings › Account. Signing out removes your synced library and settings from that device; they stay in your account and return when you sign in again.

Catalog, images and where to watch

Titles, people, search results and streaming availability come from TMDB through our own API, which Expo's EAS Hosting runs at yify.expo.app. The app sends our API what it needs to answer: the page or title requested, your search words, your country, the streaming services you filter by and the titles whose availability it shows. Our API passes these to TMDB without your IP address or account details. Your device does not send catalog requests to TMDB itself.

Our API keeps your IP address briefly in memory to limit how many requests one connection can make. It writes one log line per request with the kind of request, the result and how long it took, and never logs your IP address, search words or account details. Expo processes your IP address and the addresses you request in order to serve the website and the API, and may keep standard request logs.

On the website, our API reads the country that Expo's network derives from your IP address to suggest your starting country. The suggestion is saved on your device only, never on our servers. In the Android app, the starting country comes from your device's region setting. No version 2 of Yify asks for location permission. You can change your country in You › Settings › Region and services.

Posters, backdrops, photos and streaming-service logos load directly from TMDB's image server (image.tmdb.org), which receives your IP address and the image requested. If those images keep failing on your connection, the app loads them through our API instead for the next 24 hours.

JustWatch supplies the streaming availability that TMDB passes to us; JustWatch receives no information from Yify. Choosing a streaming service opens TMDB's watch page for that title in your browser, where links lead to each service under its own privacy policy.

Trailers and YouTube API Services

Yify uses YouTube API Services to play trailers. When you play a trailer, YouTube receives your IP address and what you watch, and may set cookies. Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Trailers play only when you tap Play trailer. The first time, the app asks you to agree to this policy and the YouTube Terms of Service before anything loads from YouTube, and it saves that choice on your device. The website uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com). You can turn the YouTube trailers setting off in You › Settings › Privacy; the app then asks again before the next trailer.

Usage analytics (optional)

Usage analytics is off until you choose Allow in the privacy prompt or turn on Usage analytics in You › Settings › Privacy. While it is on, Google Analytics for Firebase receives:

  • a random app instance ID, which is not your name, email address or account ID;
  • the screens you open and what you do there, such as opening a title (by its TMDB number), starting a trailer, adding to your watchlist, logging a watch, creating a list, opening a streaming service, sharing, signing in, viewing Yify Plus offers, the steps of a purchase, ads shown or skipped in the Android app, and changing a setting;
  • for a search, how many characters it had and how many results it found, never the words;
  • whether you have Yify Plus and whether you are signed in;
  • device and app details, such as the model, operating system, language and app version, and an approximate location (country, region and city) that Google derives from your IP address.

Analytics never receives your diary notes, the ratings you give, the names of your lists or your search words. On the website, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell visits apart; Google signals and ad personalization are turned off for our analytics, and turning analytics off stops collection and deletes those cookies. In the Android app, while analytics is on, the app shares its analytics instance ID with RevenueCat, which then reports purchases and renewals to Google Analytics. If you see ads in the Android app, Google Analytics also records ad impressions while analytics is on.

While analytics is on, Sentry also receives performance measurements and counts of expected failures, such as how long screens and requests take and how often a request timed out. These carry the same filtered details as crash reports.

Crash and error reports

To find and fix problems, the app and the website send crash and error reports to Sentry. These reports are always on, because we need them to keep Yify working; they are never used for advertising. A report contains the error and where it happened in our code, the app or browser version, the operating system, device details such as the model, memory, storage, screen, battery and network state, the kind of screen where it happened (never the title or person it showed) and a short list of the app's recent steps, such as which kind of request failed.

Before a report leaves your device, the app removes email addresses, account identifiers, search words, access tokens and the paths of web addresses. The Android app also records how it was installed (for example from Google Play) so we can tell real devices from automated test devices. Sentry receives your IP address with each report and is configured not to store it. There is no session replay, screen recording or profiling, and the app sends at most 25 error reports per session. Our API reports its own failures to Sentry with only the name of the failing request type and the data center that served it.

If you send us a problem report from the app or by email, we receive your message and anything you choose to include. Please leave out sensitive information.

Ads in the Android app

If you don't have Yify Plus, the Android app may occasionally show a full-screen Google AdMob ad when you open a title. Ads never appear when the app starts, on links you open from elsewhere, or while a trailer is playing or about to play, and the ad SDK is not started for Yify Plus members. The website shows no ads.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK receives your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires consent, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads load, and Ad privacy choices in You › Settings › Privacy lets you change them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and manage Google's ad personalization in My Ad Center. Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Yify Plus purchases

Yify Plus is a Google Play subscription. Google Play handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat validates purchases and keeps a record of your subscription and purchase history so that Plus can unlock, renew and be restored.

In the Android app you can buy Plus without an account. RevenueCat then knows you by a random app user ID created on your device. If you sign in, RevenueCat links that ID to your account ID so that Plus also works on the website. RevenueCat's collection of device identifiers is turned off. On the website, RevenueCat is contacted only when you are signed in and a page needs your Plus status, and it receives your account ID. The website does not sell Plus. Your current Plus status is saved on your device so that the app knows it offline.

App updates

The Android app uses Google Play's in-app update service to learn when a newer version is available in the store; the update downloads in the background and installs when you choose Restart or while the app is in the background. Between store releases, Expo's update service (EAS Update) can deliver small fixes: each time the app starts, it asks Expo whether one is available, sending a random installation ID that Expo uses to deliver and count updates, the app version, the update channel and your IP address.

Storage on your device and cookies

The Android app keeps your library, settings and choices in its private storage. The website keeps them in your browser's local storage, together with your signed-in session from Firebase. Yify sets no cookies of its own. Cookies can come from Google Analytics if you allow it on the website, from YouTube when you play a trailer, and from Firebase's sign-in handler, which we serve from yify.expo.app, while you sign in. These legal pages load no analytics, advertising or sign-in code.

2. Service providers

These providers process information for the purposes above, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for your account and sync; Google Analytics for Firebase for usage analytics, only with your consent.
  • Google: AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ads and ad consent in the Android app; Google Play for payments and in-app updates; YouTube for trailers.
  • Sentry: crash and error reports, and performance measurements with your consent.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation and subscription status.
  • Expo: EAS Hosting, which runs the website and our API, and EAS Update, which delivers app updates.
  • TMDB: catalog data through our API, and images loaded from its image server.
  • JustWatch: streaming availability, supplied through TMDB. It receives no information from Yify.

We may disclose information when the law requires it, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect users and the service. If Yify changes ownership, your information stays subject to this policy and to any notice the law requires.

3. Legal bases

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on these grounds:

  • Providing the service you ask for: your account and sync, Yify Plus, the catalog and trailers you open, and app updates.
  • Your consent: usage analytics and the related performance measurements, playing trailers from YouTube, and ad choices where Google's consent form asks for them. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Our legitimate interests: crash and error reports that keep Yify working, protecting the API from abuse, answering your messages, and funding the free app with occasional ads where no consent is required.
  • Legal obligations: keeping purchase records where the law requires it.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Turn Usage analytics and YouTube trailers on or off in You › Settings › Privacy. These choices are saved on each device and are not synced.
  • In the Android app, use Ad privacy choices in You › Settings › Privacy when Google's consent form applies in your region, or get Yify Plus to turn off Yify ads.
  • Use Yify without signing in, sign out at any time, pause or clear Recently viewed in your Library, and remove any title, diary entry or list.
  • Delete your account in You › Settings › Account › Delete account, or follow the account deletion instructions.
  • Ask for a copy of your data with Request a copy of your data in You › Settings › Privacy, which opens an email to us, or email kunal26das@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email us to use these rights. We may ask you to confirm that you control the account concerned; we never ask for your password. We answer without undue delay and within one month; if a request is complex and we need up to two more months, we tell you within the first month. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can also nominate someone to exercise your rights after death or incapacity, and please contact us first with any grievance.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: until you remove it, sign out (for synced data), clear the app's or the browser's data, or uninstall the app.
  • In your account: until you delete it or your account. A removed title or entry leaves a small marker without its notes or ratings so that your other devices remove it too; watchlist and recently viewed markers are dropped after 90 days.
  • Account deletion removes your Firebase account and everything synced to it, including data saved by Yify 1.8 or earlier, from our servers and from the device you delete it on. An empty deletion marker under your former account ID stays so that other signed-in devices cannot restore deleted data; it holds no titles, dates, ratings or notes. See Delete your account.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat keeps your purchase history while we use it; email us to have your RevenueCat record deleted. Google Play keeps its transaction records under its own policy and legal duties. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Analytics and crash reports are not linked to your account. They are kept according to our retention settings with Google Analytics and Sentry, then deleted.
  • Messages you send us are kept as long as we need them to handle your request.

6. Security and international processing

Information travels over encrypted connections, and the Cloud Firestore rules let only your signed-in account read or change your synced data. No system is completely secure. Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, under their data-protection agreements and the safeguards the law requires.

7. Children

Yify is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Yify 1.8 and earlier (Android)

Until every Android user has moved to version 2, Yify 1.8 and earlier stay installed on some devices. Besides what this policy describes, those versions:

  • report crashes to Firebase Crashlytics as well as Sentry;
  • ask for approximate location permission, only when you choose to detect your viewing country, and keep only the resulting country;
  • check for new titles in the background and show local notifications if you turn them on;
  • send a launch event with app and device details to Expo's EAS Insights;
  • load their movie catalog from third-party catalog services through our legacy API, which receives the pages and titles you request and your IP address.

Updating to version 2 from Google Play stops all of these. We will remove this section once version 2 has replaced the older versions.
