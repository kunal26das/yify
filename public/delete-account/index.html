Your account

Delete your account

You can delete your Yify account and the data synced to it at any time. Deletion is permanent.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open Yify on Android or at yify.expo.app, and sign in with the Google account you use for Yify.
  2. Go to You › Settings › Account › Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and choose Delete account.
  4. Google asks you to confirm your account. If you cancel here, nothing changes.

When deletion finishes you see "Your account was deleted." and you are signed out. If you have Yify Plus, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play first, in Payments & subscriptions.

If you can't use the app

Email kunal26das@gmail.com from the Google account you use for Yify and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to confirm that you control the account; we never ask for your password. We act on deletion requests within 30 days.

What deletion removes

  • Your sign-in account: your account ID, name, email address and profile photo.
  • Everything synced to the account: your watchlist, your diary with its dates, ratings and notes, your lists, your recently viewed titles and your synced settings.
  • Data that Yify 1.8 and earlier saved to the account: the old watchlist, history, collections, journal and settings.
  • The same synced data on the device you delete your account from.

What remains

  • An empty deletion marker under your former account ID. It stops other signed-in devices from restoring deleted data, holds no titles, dates, ratings or notes, and cannot be changed.
  • Your purchase record at RevenueCat. Email us to have it deleted. Google Play keeps its own records of your purchases under its policy and legal duties.
  • Other devices. A device that is still signed in to the deleted account stops syncing and shows that the account was deleted. Sign out there to remove its copy.
  • Data saved only on a device, such as a library kept without signing in, recent searches and privacy choices. It is not part of your account. Clear the app's data or uninstall it on Android, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports. They are not linked to your account and expire under our retention settings. Email us if you want to ask about them.

If deletion stops partway

If the last step fails, for example because the connection drops, your synced data has already been removed and You › Settings › Account shows Finish deleting account, which repeats only that step.
