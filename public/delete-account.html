Delete your Yify account

You can delete your Yify account and everything synced to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Yify and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm when asked. Your account and its synced data are deleted straight away.

You need to be signed in to see this option. If you have signed out, sign back in with the same Google account first.

Delete it without the app

If you no longer have Yify installed, email the developer contact address shown on the Yify listing on Google Play from the Google account you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that same email address before deleting anything.

What is deleted

DataWhen
Your account record — email address, name, profile photo and account IDImmediately
Your synced watchlist, including the record of titles you removedImmediately
Your synced settings — theme, browse defaults, playback and notification preferencesImmediately

What is kept, and for how long

DataWhy and for how long
Purchase records Held by Google Play and our billing provider for accounting, tax and refund obligations. Deleting your Yify account does not cancel or refund a purchase — manage those through Google Play.
Anonymous usage and crash diagnostics Never linked to your account — Yify does not attach your account ID to analytics or crash reports. Retained under Google’s standard retention periods and cannot be traced back to a deleted account.
Anything saved only on your device Your local watchlist and recent searches stay on the device. Clear them from Settings, or remove them by uninstalling the app.
