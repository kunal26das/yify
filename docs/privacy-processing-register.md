Working register for Yify 2.0 (Android and `https://yify.expo.app`). Responsible owner: Kunal Das, operating as Kudos Labs; contact `kunal26das@gmail.com`; correspondence address as published in `public/privacy.html`. Audience: people aged 13 and over (Play target audience 13+, owner action O-8 in `docs/v2/SPEC.md`). The public notice is `public/privacy.html`; it lists the same providers. Keep the two in step: a processing change updates both in the same pull request, and `tests/scripts/legal-pages.test.cjs` checks that the policy names every provider below.

## Processing decisions

These are proposed GDPR grounds, not findings that every activity meets them. "Requested service" means data necessary for a feature the person chooses; it does not make advertising, measurement or every SDK operation necessary. Cookie and device-access rules (ePrivacy) need their own assessment even where GDPR permits the processing.

Approve each row with its data categories, recipients, necessity, ground and evidence reference. Do not infer sensitive traits from viewing history; diary notes can contain unexpected sensitive information and must stay out of telemetry (analytics carries only `has_note`, never the text). Assess EU representative requirements separately. Controller identity and contact details are required; offering more than one contact channel is good practice under the [transparency guidance](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/article29/document.cfm?action=display&doc_id=51025), but a postal address is not a universal prerequisite.

## Retention evidence and unresolved periods

| Data | Observed behaviour; decision still needed |
| --- | --- |
| Device data | Kept until the person removes it, signs out (synced sections and preferences are cleared, unsynced edits go to a per-account stash), clears app or site data, or uninstalls. Yify 1.x keys are deleted 30 days after the v2 migration (`v2.migration.v1CleanupAt`). |
| Account content | Kept for the account features until removal or account deletion. Watchlist and recent tombstones are pruned after 90 days; diary and list tombstones keep no notes, ratings or items. Firestore backups and recovery windows are unverified. |
| Deleted-account barrier | `journals/{uid}` keeps an empty payload, the deletion flag and a timestamp. The rules stop stale clients restoring content. There is no automatic expiry; document proportionality and a safe retirement condition before introducing a purge. |
| Purchases | RevenueCat keeps customer and transaction history while the project exists; customer deletion happens on request (owner action). Google Play keeps its own records. |
| Analytics, diagnostics and ads | GA4 retention setting, Sentry plan retention and Google's ad data periods are configured in the consoles; record the actual values here before publishing specific periods. Withdrawing consent stops future optional collection; it is not proof of retroactive erasure. |
| Hosting logs | EAS Hosting keeps console output and request logs under Expo's retention; the API never writes IPs, query text or tokens to them. |

## Requests and grievances

1. Record the receipt time, requested right, jurisdiction, owner and deadline in a restricted register. Use signed-in ownership or a proportionate check; never ask for passwords, routine ID scans or unrelated viewing details. Requests come through the published email (the app's "Request a copy of your data" row opens one); no special phrase is required. `docs/privacy-requests.md` describes the export tool.
2. GDPR: act without undue delay, normally within one month. If complexity or volume justifies up to two further months, explain within the first month. **Proposed internal grievance target: 30 days, pending owner approval.** Once applicable, publish a DPDP grievance period of no more than 90 days; it does not replace shorter applicable deadlines. [EDPB rights guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en), [DPDP Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).
3. An access or export response covers Firebase, purchases, telemetry and support records plus the purposes, recipients and retention context. A Firebase export is one component, not a complete response. Review third-party data and deliver securely. Explain unavailable records and real limits, such as analytics keyed only by an app instance ID; do not treat correction, objection, restriction, portability and erasure as the same request.
4. Erasure follows the in-app order: barrier first, then sections and `users/{uid}`, then the Auth user, then RevenueCat on request. Document any narrowly justified retention and its purge condition, collect receipts and remove temporary exports after delivery. Account deletion does not cancel billing. Escalate a failed cleanup instead of closing the request as successful.
5. Nomination (DPDP): accept a request through the same contact, verify the account holder, record the nominee's minimum necessary name, contact and scope securely, and offer a correction and revocation route. Disclose nothing until death or incapacity and the nominee's authority are proportionately verified. This procedure needs owner and legal approval and a secure record before it is advertised as operational.
6. Language: record the person's requested English or Eighth Schedule language and arrange a reviewed translation of the notice before any processing that depends on consent given in that language. DPDP requires exhausting the grievance route before the Board; GDPR complaints to an authority do not depend on that procedure. [DPDP Act, sections 5–6 and 11–14](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf).

## Breach response

Contain access, preserve the evidence needed, record the time of awareness, the people and data affected, the consequences and the mitigations, and involve the owner at once. Assess notifying the GDPR authority within 72 hours unless the breach is unlikely to risk people's rights and freedoms, and notify individuals without undue delay when the risk is high. Once DPDP rules apply, notify the Board and affected people without delay and send the further Board details within 72 hours unless extended. Keep the decision records, including a justified decision not to notify, and verify recipients and facts before sending. Do not wait for a final root-cause report. [GDPR Articles 33–34](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), [DPDP Rule 7](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

Core DPDP duties are scheduled for **13 May 2027**; GDPR already applies where its territorial scope is met, and existing Indian duties continue meanwhile ([MeitY confirmation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2261823&lang=1&reg=3)). Owner sign-off, provider evidence and verification of the live controls remain open actions.
